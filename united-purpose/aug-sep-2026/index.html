How "contact rate" is calculated, and where it might be off

This number comes entirely from Surefire's Lifecycle Status field (New → Attempted Contact → Made Contact → Consultation Scheduled → Application Sent → Application Started → Nurturing), as pulled into Ethan's biweekly export. As of this check-in, "contact rate" means Made Contact or further, an actual conversation, not just Attempted Contact. That's a stricter bar than prior check-ins used, and I recomputed the Aug 20 baseline under this same definition for a fair comparison.

I don't have a confirmed update on the Surefire "Failed to Contact" status and attempt-count tracking discussed on the 8/6 call, so a 0% could still mean "talked to them but didn't log the stage change" rather than "never reached." That caveat matters more now than it did under the looser "attempted" definition, since a missed status update has a bigger effect on the number.

Two honest caveats before reading too much into this

This is confounded by which LO the lead landed with, not just lead quality. Until the follow-up gap closes for the reps still flat, any keyword or ad-set "opportunity rate" is really measuring ad quality tangled up with which rep got the lead. Don't make budget calls off this table alone yet.

The email join keeps surfacing duplicate CRM contacts, 7 this pull (same person submitting twice, sometimes assigned to two different LOs). Corrected by keeping the furthest stage across duplicates each time, same fix as Aug 20.
