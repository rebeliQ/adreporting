Facebook ● Medium Priority
Monitor Georgia + MD Expansion (30 Days)
GA added Sep 10 — 6 GA leads in first 28 days with no MD leads yet. Price intent is strong (100% of FB leads targeted $200K+ homes), but credit quality remains structural at 7% closeable. Give GA/MD 30 days of data before deciding to scale.
Facebook ● High Priority
Add a Disqualifying Gate to the Credit Score Question
The form already asks estimated credit score — 135 of 180 leads self-reported below 620 and submitted anyway. The question is collecting data but not filtering. The fix is a disqualifying gate: if a lead selects below 620, block submission and redirect to a credit repair resource. This cleans the CRM pipeline directly — and since Housing Special Ad Category blocks audience-level targeting, the form gate is the only lever left.
